How does one solve the challenge of having too many notebooks?  

For many, notebooks are a _conduit_ to ideas. Yes, the ones that pop into our heads, from time to time. But, ultimately, the ones that are forged through the friction between pen and paper.  

We buy many notebooks because they are a tool. Very much like a sharp knife to a chef, or the right kind of cleat to a striker.  

And therein lies the "problem". The perfect idea, that precious atom of brilliance we spend a lifetime searching for, cannot be born from anything else other than the perfect trace, on the perfect page, with the perfect stroke.  

Or can it?  

The truth is ideas are seldom brilliant in and by themselves. What ends up making an idea, any idea, good is contact with reality. Clashing with constraints. Collaborating with others through feedback and disagreement. Forking, merging, and iterating. And none of that can happen as long as an idea remains a prisioner of the mind.  

So, how do you solve the challenge of having too many notebooks?  

Just. Write.
