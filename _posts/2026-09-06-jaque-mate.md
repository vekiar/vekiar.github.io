Three sets of three rules each.  
Opening:
1. Control the center
2. Develop pieces
3. Castle! [1]

Midgame:
1. Attack Weaknesses
2. Improve Your Position
3. Identify the Threat (and avoid mistakes!) [2]

Endgame:
1. Activate the King
2. Attack the pawns
3. Get a passed pawn

Here be dragons:  
Do not create spaces for your opponent that are not there in the first place.  

Notes:  
[1] This rule is best defined as "protect the king", with some lines of thought arguing that castling may not always be the best move (!research).
[2] Possibly the [most important thing in chess](link to "winning at chess is the art of making the second to last mistake")).
