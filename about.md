# Social Contract
This is a space for ideas. Ideas represent a peculiar point-in-time understanding of the world, and convery a principled perspective. You are free to disagree with anything you read here. You are encouraged to provide feedback and challenge ideas where you believe it appropriate.  

# AI Use Policy
All writing will be original human writing. AI will be used for brainstorming, research, and development. Where it is used, it will be evident. Unless there is a good reason not to, conversations (prompts, reasoning if available, output) will be made available.
